## Read

**GO for Phase 2 — with the central caveat made concrete.** Rank IC is positive and HAC-significant at multiple horizons (the *typical* cheap bond converges: monotone median returns, top-minus-bottom median LS = +0.0022). But the equal-weight *mean* long-short is NEGATIVE (-0.0051) because ~12% of the cheapest bucket are distressed names with catastrophic returns. The signal has genuine convergence content; an uncontrolled portfolio loses to the distressed tail. This is exactly what Phase 2 (liquidity controls, log-spread) and Phase 3 (default carry-through, contamination test, neutralization) are built to fix.
